The 14-Year Journey of Vance Dental Studio

2012 • The Founding
2 Chairs on Far West Blvd
Dr. Vance opened the practice with just two operatories and a single hygienist, committed to spending a full 60 minutes with every patient to address dental fear.
2016 • Pediatric & Family Expansion
Introducing "Mr. Tooth" & Anxiety Protocols
Introduced topical micro-numbing gels and our friendly mascot "Mr. Tooth" to eliminate needle anxiety for young children and nervous adults alike.
2020 • High-Tech Modernization

Replaced uncomfortable silicone impression putty with low-radiation 3D optical scanning, enabling single-day digital crowns and computer-guided implants.
