## 1. Project Goal & Core Deliverables
Build a high-conversion, sub-second-loading mobile website designed specifically for Denver homeowners experiencing HVAC or furnace breakdowns during winter freezing temperatures.

### Deliverables:
1. **Emergency Hero & Tap-to-Call System:**
   - Sticky 24/7 Emergency Dispatch bar with click-to-call: `(303) 578-2910`.
   - "Technician at your door in under 60 minutes" reassurance badge.
2. **Transparent Diagnostic & Repair Pricing:**
   - $89 Emergency Diagnostic Special (waived with any completed repair).
   - Clear price ranges for common repairs (igniters, blower motors, heat exchangers).
   - High-efficiency replacement packages with 0% APR financing calculators.
3. **Interactive Dispatch Request Form:**
   - 30-second mobile form for non-urgent service bookings and estimates.
4. **Trust & Compliance Signals:**
   - NATE-Certified logo badges, EPA Universal certification, and BBB A+ status.
   - Genuine personal note from Owner Brett Callahan explaining Denver freeze protection.
5. **Local Denver SEO & Neighborhood Coverage:**
   - Schema markup for local HVAC contractor in Denver, Lakewood, Aurora, Littleton, and Arvada.
